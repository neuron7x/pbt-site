# PLAINBUD · Industrial Exactitude · editorial contract

Status: draft editorial implementation, 2026-11-23. Scope: language, semantics, positioning and the language of risk. This is not a declaration of business readiness.

## The positioning decision

Audience: general contractors, site supervisors, procurement staff and project owners who need machinery for unusual site conditions. These are buyer-role hypotheses, not interview findings.

Core proposition: **«Важкі умови. Техніка під об’єкт.»** It names the difficulty without claiming market supremacy. Below the headline, immediately name the work: waterways, earthworks, lifting and oversized transport.

Personality: disciplined industrial, not theatrical aggression. Short, physical nouns; hard stops; specific machine classes, constraints, decisions and actions. The intimidating fact is a clear technical specification. An unsupported superlative is a liability, not confidence.

Three evaluated territories:
1. **Industrial Exactitude — adopted.** Hard contrasts and exact nouns. «Важкі умови. Техніка під об’єкт.» Strong and credible if copy distinguishes catalog from confirmed availability.
2. **Machine Brutalism — reserve.** «Ґрунт. Вода. Негабарит.» Graphic, minimal; may fail to identify the service above the fold. Use only as eyebrow/visual composition.
3. **Procurement Clarity — supporting system.** «Параметри → попередній розрахунок → запит → погодження». Useful around calculator and form but too generic for the primary brand voice.

## Page grammar

Every block should answer: (1) what class of problem, (2) what technical mechanism or equipment, (3) what can actually be substantiated, (4) what remains conditional, (5) the exact next action. Paragraphs: one substantial claim each, preferably under 30 words when legibility permits. Avoid slogans without a referent, fake urgency, «унікальні рішення», «професіонали своєї справи» and numerical boastfulness without denominator.

The words **«власний»**, **«є в наявності»**, **«усі сертифікати»**, **«працюємо 24/7»**, **«найкращі»**, **«гарантуємо виїзд»** are not stylistic options. They are evidence-gated business claims.

Catalog quantities are facts **about this source file**, not about the physical fleet. User-facing prices are historical scenarios, NOT current quotes. The material-document entry is a request for batch-specific evidence, not a universal compliance warranty. Footer disclaimer cannot rescue a misleading headline.

## Conversion language

- Hero → **«Надіслати запит»**. Accurate action: lead request, not reservation.
- Calculator → **«Додати параметри до заявки»**. It fills the form; it does not place an order.
- Site form → state the next stage and an honest limitation: no confirmation of rate, availability or dispatch date.
- Fallback and success → continue using actual transport/SQLite states, never promise that a human read the lead after only a server acknowledgement.
- Keep field labels, values, input names, accessible names, statuses and links coherent; never change API enum values for copy.

## Search and accessibility

Explicit topical nouns in first paragraphs, navigation and metadata: оренда спецтехніки, екскаватор-амфібія, автокран, трал, земляні роботи, очищення водойм, щебінь. Do not keyword-stuff or generate doorway pages for oblasts. A city+category page becomes warranted only with verified local operations and genuinely unique content.

Preserve heading order, accessible names, keyboard targets and all visible constraints next to calculator/submit controls. Ensure legibility with both existing themes and reduced-motion static profile. Pure text changes still require screenshot checks at all viewport widths.

## Evidence hierarchy and validity limits

1. Dated owner records, signed tariff/availability documents, per-asset provenance and real CRM outcome receipts are primary for PLAINBUD business assertions. Registry identity cannot prove operational capacity. The current owner-supplied company card is not an independent register check.
2. Source-code tests prove mechanics of this revision, not business truth or conversion uplift.
3. WCAG 2.2 normative web accessibility: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ ; verify against actual contrast and interactions. Automated scans alone never certify full conformance.
4. Google Search Central: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content ; helpful content principles are guidance, NOT a guaranteed rank or conversion effect.
5. Ukrainian advertising law: https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/270/96-%D0%B2%D1%80#Text ; privacy law: https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2297-17#Text . Apply effective law and qualified legal review; no agent-generated copy constitutes legal clearance.
6. Nielsen Norman Group / Baymard may inform task-focused information design, but general UX studies do not establish causal uplift for Ukrainian construction procurement.
7. Competitor copy is research input, never training material to paraphrase. Originality requires editorial comparison and human review; a simple plagiarism detector cannot prove uniqueness.

## Evidence gate and release

The machine-checkable ledger is claim-ledger.json; controlled terminology is terminology.json; experiment register is experiment-register.json. Any external claim without an owner receipt remains BLOCKED. A new positive claim must include a unique ID, source, authority, scope, verifiable definition, owner, verified and expiry dates, fallback text, and a manually reviewed public surface. Do not promote by changing the string in the ledger alone.

For low-traffic B2B CRO: interview buyers first, preregister randomization if feasible, log CRM qualification outcomes under existing consent, report uncertainty, and refuse causality claims from clicks. No analytics dependencies or trackers are added in this change.

Owners still must resolve docs/PRODUCTION_HOLD.md, including tariffs, taxes, rights, retention, domain and authorized CRM receiver tests. A green code gate is not permission to publish.
